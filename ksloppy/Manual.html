kSloppy

Open a document. Press Ctrl+Alt+S to open the shape workspace. Draw a rough shape over the artwork preview, then press Enter to perfect it. Choose a shape from the dropdown when automatic detection guesses wrong. Press Ctrl+Enter to apply your shapes as an editable vector layer.

Draw another stroke after perfecting the previous one to keep both. Backspace removes the current stroke or the last staged shape. Scroll to zoom, middle-drag to pan. Closing asks before discarding unapplied work.

This is a separate drawing workspace, not a filter for ordinary paint strokes. Supports circles, ellipses, lines, circular arcs, rectangles, squares, irregular polygons, and regular polygons with 3–64 sides. All shapes in a batch share styling. Delete the named kSloppy layer to remove an applied batch.
